OptiUniverse turns your iPhone or iPad into an interactive 3D solar-system viewer. Move through a real-time Metal scene, discover celestial destinations, and inspect each world with concise science facts and orbital details.

Built for people who love astronomy, space visuals, and hands-on exploration, OptiUniverse combines a polished SwiftUI discovery experience with high-fidelity Metal rendering on device.

Features:
- Explore a real-time 3D solar-system scene
- Discover planets, moons, gas giants, ice giants, and distant worlds
- Pan, pinch, rotate, and follow celestial objects with smooth camera controls
- View destination cards with distance, diameter, orbital period, temperature, and short descriptions
- Visualize transfer-orbit routes for supported destinations
- Enjoy detailed textured materials, HDR rendering, bloom, tone mapping, and star-field visuals

OptiUniverse is designed for quick discovery and immersive inspection, whether you want to browse the solar system casually or appreciate the rendering details of each celestial object.
